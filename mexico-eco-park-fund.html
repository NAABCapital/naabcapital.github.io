Geographic Focus

The Yucatan Peninsula — A Region on the Rise

Massive federal infrastructure investment is unleashing structural demand for sustainable real estate. The Fund captures this opportunity before market saturation.

Yucatan Peninsula
Tulum International Airport · Mexico's First Green Airport
01

Mayan Train

1,500 km rail across 5 states and 16 stations, passing 14 Magical Towns, 6 UNESCO World Heritage Sites, and 50+ archaeological sites — generating 3M+ annual passengers through the region.

02

Tulum International Airport

Mexico's first "Green Airport," with a 3.7km hydraulic concrete runway serving narrow, wide-body, and private aircraft. Projected to draw 5.5M+ annual passengers.

03

Jaguar National Park

2,600+ hectare sanctuary (~3x Central Park) protecting local wildlife. Draws 2.4M+ annual visitors and anchors the region's eco-tourism identity.
